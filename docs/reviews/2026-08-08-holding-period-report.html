The three questions this run was built to answer

1. How does directional accuracy vary with holding period?

Not monotonically, and not usefully. 6h 61% (CI 46–74, n=41); 24h 32% (CI 20–47, n=41); 72h 44% (CI 30–59, n=41); 168h 46% (CI 32–61, n=41); 336h 39% (CI 26–54, n=41); 720h 27% (CI 16–42, n=41). The best is 6h at 61%, the worst 720h at 27%. 4 of 6 intervals contain 50%, and only 1 horizon(s) beat the better constant strategy on the same calls (6h by +4.9 pp), which is well inside the interval width. Conclusion: the horizons cannot be ranked at this sample size.

2. Where is expected value highest, and does it agree with accuracy?

By mean R under the decision's own stop and target, the top horizon is 336h at +0.17R; by mean blind-hold return it is 168h at +0.95%. Neither matches the accuracy peak at 6h. Every mean R sits between +0.00 and +0.17R — a spread far smaller than the per-trade dispersion. At 3 horizon(s) the mean and the median disagree in sign (72h mean +0.47% vs median -0.30%, 168h mean +0.95% vs median -0.64%, 336h mean +0.85% vs median -1.65%), so those means are carried by a handful of trades, not by the typical one. Conclusion: no horizon has a demonstrable expected-value advantage.

3. Do the stops match the horizon the decisions are aimed at?

No. Stops are placed 0.39–6.18% from the intended entry (mean 2.68%), while the mean adverse excursion is 6h 1.26%; 24h 2.00%; 72h 2.72%; 168h 3.82%; 336h 5.44%; 720h 9.69%. By 168h, 8 of 15 market-entry calls would already have hit their stop, and 4 of those had the direction right at the horizon. The stop is sized for hours; the excursions it has to survive are sized for days. Conclusion: the stop widths are mismatched to any horizon beyond about a day — but since no horizon shows an edge, widening the stop alone would not turn this profitable.

Two measurements, deliberately. Blind hold enters at the first bar after the decision and holds to the h-hour mark with no stop and no target — it measures the direction call alone, which is the only thing comparable across columns. Race applies the decision's own stop and target and marks to market at the horizon if neither is hit. Where the two disagree, the tactics, not the analysis, are what moved the number.

Method and its limits

  • Point in time. The data layer is frozen at each cutoff: a bar is visible only once close_time <= as_of, and live HTTP is replaced by a raiser, so a leak fails the run instead of contaminating it silently.
  • Market analyst only. News, Reddit and the newsflash relay have no dated archive. This measures selected_analysts=["market"], not the three-analyst production configuration.
  • Overlapping forward windows. The 24 windows span 2026-01-11 → 2026-07-08; at 336h and 720h their forward paths overlap, so those rows carry far less independent information than their n suggests. See eff. indep..
  • Race R at a horizon is realised when the stop or target is hit and marked to market otherwise, so it is not directly comparable with a pure win/loss R.
  • Excluded decisions are listed in the matrix with their reason and are absent from every statistic; they are not counted as losses.
